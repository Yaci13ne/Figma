# 🍕 FoodGo – Food Delivery & Discovery App

## 🎯 Goal
Design a modern and user-friendly **mobile interface** for a food delivery and restaurant discovery platform.  
**FoodGo** allows users to **browse restaurants**, **explore menu items**, and **order food** seamlessly through an intuitive, visually appealing UI.  
The main objective is to create a **fast and satisfying experience** that makes food discovery and ordering as easy as possible.

---

## 🧩 Design Overview
- **Platform:** Mobile (iOS-style layout)
- **Theme:** Clean & appetizing food-focused design
- **Primary Colors:**
  - Background: White and light gray for cleanliness
  - Accent: Warm colors to stimulate appetite
  - Text: Dark for readability with secondary gray for details
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif for modern, readable interface
- **Visual Style:** Rounded cards, food imagery, clear CTAs

---

## 🧭 User Flow

### 1. **Onboarding & Welcome**
- Brand introduction ("Go get your favorite bite")
- Simple get started CTA
- App preview showcasing food variety

### 2. **Home & Discovery**
- Personalized greeting ("Welcome to FoodGo")
- Promotional banners (50% off deals)
- Category filters (Pizza, Burger, Sushi, Soup)
- Grid layout for food items with:
  - Food images
  - Item names and prices
  - Quick order options

### 3. **Food Detail Page**
- Large food image
- Item name, price, and description
- Ingredients and customization options
- Prominent "Add to Cart" CTA

### 4. **Cart & Checkout**
- Itemized order summary with quantities
- Subtotal and shipping calculations
- Gift card/discount code application
- Secure checkout process

### 5. **Order Tracking & History**
- Real-time delivery status
- Delivery partner information
- Order history with ratings
- Delivery address and fee details

### 6. **User Profile**
- Account information and editing
- Delivery address management
- Favorite items tracking
- Notification preferences
- Order history

---

## 💡 Design Decisions
- **Clean white background:** Creates sense of hygiene and professionalism
- **High-quality food imagery:** Stimulates appetite and helps decision making
- **Prominent pricing:** Clear cost transparency throughout the journey
- **Streamlined navigation:** Bottom tab bar for essential sections (Home, Cart, Profile)
- **Progressive disclosure:** Simple to complex information hierarchy

---

## 🧠 UX Considerations
- **Quick category access:** Reduces decision fatigue for common cravings
- **Visual food presentation:** Images help users make informed choices
- **Streamlined ordering:** Minimal steps from discovery to checkout
- **Order tracking:** Provides transparency and reduces anxiety
- **Personalization:** Favorites and order history for returning users

---

## ⚙️ Features (Prototype)
- Interactive food browsing and category filtering
- Detailed product pages with customization options
- Shopping cart with real-time price updates
- Order tracking with delivery partner information
- User profile with preferences and history
- Search functionality across restaurants and menu items

---

## 🚀 Future Improvements
- Add **real-time delivery tracking** with map integration
- Include **personalized recommendations** based on order history
- Add **group ordering** features for shared meals
- Integrate **loyalty programs** and rewards
- Include **dietary preference filters** (vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.)
- Add **scheduled ordering** for future deliveries
