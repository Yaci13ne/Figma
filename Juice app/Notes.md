# 🍹 JUICE – Fresh & Natural Drink App

## 🎯 Goal
Design a **fresh, colorful, and dynamic mobile experience** for a juice brand that promotes natural drinks made from real fruits.  
The goal is to build an interface that feels **refreshing, minimal, and vibrant**, emphasizing each juice’s unique flavor and personality through visual storytelling and subtle animation.

---

## 🧩 Design Overview
- **Platform:** Mobile (iOS-style layout)
- **Theme:** Bright & fruity color palette representing nature and freshness
- **Primary Colors:**
  - Orange: `#D8A22E` (energy, vitality)
  - Green: `#8EA32C` (freshness, natural)
  - Red: `#F36B6C` (sweetness, liveliness)
  - Plum: `#CC98A9` (richness, elegance)
- **Typography:** Rounded, friendly sans-serif font for an organic and modern look  
- **Visual Style:** Centered bottle display, smooth transitions, fruit-themed icons

---

## 🧭 User Flow

### 1. **Home / Cover Page**
- Brand logo with fruit icon
- Showcases 4 main juices: **Orange, Plum, Kiwi, Strawberry**
- Split background with contrasting tones (orange–green) for visual balance
- Animated bottles displayed to introduce the brand and guide user attention
- Minimal navigation icons at the bottom for flavor categories

### 2. **Juice Detail Page (Example: Kiwi Juice)**
- Dedicated page for each juice flavor  
- Large juice bottle centered on screen  
- Accompanying fruit photo for sensory appeal  
- Short, vivid description emphasizing flavor and freshness  
- “Next” button for animated navigation between flavors  
- Smooth vertical and horizontal transitions between juices  

---

## 💡 Design Decisions
- **Fruit-based color coding:** Each flavor uses its dominant natural color for instant recognition.  
- **Minimal layout:** Focuses attention on the product (bottle and fruit image).  
- **Center-aligned composition:** Creates balance and aesthetic focus on each juice.  
- **Soft typography & icons:** Reinforce the organic, friendly brand tone.  
- **Motion design:** Simple animations (bottle transitions and next button) add playfulness and delight.  

---

## 🧠 UX Considerations
- **Flavor navigation:** “Next” button + bottom fruit icons make it easy to switch between juices.  
- **Color psychology:** Warm and cool tones alternate to maintain freshness and avoid monotony.  
- **Descriptive microcopy:** Simple phrases (e.g., “100% natural”) reinforce brand authenticity.  
- **Visual hierarchy:** Product always remains the central visual element.

---

## ⚙️ Features (Prototype)
- Smooth **bottle transition animation** between juices  
- Interactive navigation through flavors  
- Scrollable **fruit category icons**  
- Optimized for **852×393 px** mobile frame (iPhone 13 Mini)  
- Designed fully in **Figma** with animation frames and smart transitions  

---

## 🚀 Future Improvements
- Add **purchase and delivery** flow (e.g., "Add to Cart")  
- Include **nutritional info cards** for each flavor  
- Implement a **“Create Your Mix”** feature for custom blends  
- Introduce **sound or vibration feedback** for enhanced sensory feel
