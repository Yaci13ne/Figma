# 📚 BOOK – Book Discovery & Selling app

## 🎯 Goal
Design an elegant and intuitive **mobile interface** for a digital bookstore and reading platform.  
**BOOK** allows users to **discover books**, **explore authors**, and **purchase titles** easily through a clean, warm, and human-centered UI.  
The main objective is to create a **calm and inviting experience**, resembling the feeling of browsing books in a cozy library or café.

---

## 🧩 Design Overview
- **Platform:** Mobile (iOS-style layout)
- **Theme:** Light & warm for a comfortable reading mood
- **Primary Colors:**
  - Background: `#FAF3E0` (soft beige)
  - Accent: `#E07A5F` (warm coral)
  - Text: `#040404` (primary), `#6B6B6B` (secondary)
- **Typography:** Serif for titles (classic book vibe) + Sans-serif for body (modern readability)
- **Visual Style:** Rounded corners, minimalist icons, balanced white space

---

## 🧭 User Flow

### 1. **Home Page**
- Personalized greeting (e.g., *Hello, Ali Space*)
- Search bar with filter and search icons
- Horizontal **category selector** (Kids, Romance, Classic, Fantasy…)
- Grid layout for book cards:
  - Book cover  
  - Title, author, price  
  - Rating and favorites icon  

### 2. **Author Detail Page**
- Prominent author portrait  
- Name, birth–death dates  
- "About" section with short biography  
- “Famous Books & Poems” list for quick navigation  

### 3. **Book Detail Page**
- Large book cover preview  
- Title, author, publication year, price, and rating  
- Short summary under an “About” section  
- “Add to basket” button for quick purchase action

---

## 💡 Design Decisions
- **Beige background:** Mimics paper and provides a soft, readable contrast.  
- **Warm accent color:** Creates a welcoming tone and guides the eye to primary actions.  
- **Rounded cards and shadows:** Give depth and tactile feel, similar to holding a physical book.  
- **Fixed bottom navigation bar:** Keeps main actions (Home, Favorites, Basket, Profile) easily accessible.  
- **Consistent spacing:** Enhances legibility and gives a calm browsing rhythm.  

---

## 🧠 UX Considerations
- **Prominent Search:** Encourages fast discovery and filtering of titles.  
- **Author and Book Detail Hierarchy:** Smooth transition between browsing, reading bios, and purchasing.  
- **Favorites & Basket icons:** Support quick emotional engagement and intuitive shopping behavior.  
- **Readable fonts:** Serif + Sans pairing evokes literature while maintaining UI clarity.

---

## ⚙️ Features (Prototype)
- Interactive transitions between **Home → Author → Book**
- Clickable **heart** and **basket** icons
- Scrollable **categories** and **book grid**
- Optimized layout for **852×393 px** frame (iPhone 13 Mini / SE style)
- Supports both **light and warm-beige themes**

---

## 🚀 Future Improvements
